# Salty

A cheeky, witty Australian axolotl who lives in a realistic aquarium in a cosy loungeroom.

This repository is separate from Veridity and from `D:\MuseTest`. Nothing here ships into Veridity.

## First vertical slice

See Salty -> interact with her -> get a reply that fits what was said -> hear it (Pocket TTS where supported) -> watch her react.

The aquarium has to feel alive: water, light, plants, bubbles, Salty moving on her own. Not a chat window with an axolotl sticker on it.

## Who she is (look)

- Pale-pink body, speckled snout
- Feathery hot-pink external gills
- Large glossy eyes
- Delicate hands and feet
- Translucent tail
- Mischievous and expressive

## Who she is (voice)

The existing style spec is `D:\MuseTest\experiments\salty\STYLE.md` (read it, don't copy it in wholesale). In short: Australian, dry, deadpan, funny by showing rather than announcing. No catchphrases, no forced swearing, no invented life story; she never claims to be a person.
