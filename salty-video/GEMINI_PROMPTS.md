## How to make each clip

1. In Gemini, start a video and **attach `salty-start-still.png`** as the starting image. This keeps her looking the same in every clip.
2. Paste the **base text** below, then the **action** line for the clip you're making.
3. Download it and save it into `D:\MuseTest\salty-video\raw\` named after the clip (e.g. `idle_listen.mp4`).
4. Tell me, and I'll cut it into a loop and check it the same way as the first five.

Do the **must-haves first**: Gemini limits how many videos you can make per day on most plans.

If you can use Google Flow's "Frames to Video", set `salty-start-still.png` as **both** the first and last frame. Then every clip loops perfectly and I don't have to bounce it. I haven't checked whether your account has this.

### 1. idle_listen

```
Action: She sits still and listens, mouth closed with a small content smile. She breathes gently, blinks slowly twice, and her feathery gills ripple softly. A tiny head tilt to one side and back. Calm and attentive, as if listening to someone just off camera.
```

### 2. think

```
Action: She looks up and to one side, thinking, mouth pressed into a small pout. She taps her chin with one little hand, narrows her eyes as an idea forms, then looks back at the camera with a small knowing smile.
```

### 3. sympathy

```
Action: Her face softens into gentle concern: brows tilt up, eyes go soft and warm, a small closed-mouth sympathetic smile. She leans slightly toward the camera, gills droop a little, and gives a slow understanding nod. Caring, not crying.
```

### 4. mock_outrage

```
Action: She gasps in mock outrage: eyes go wide, mouth drops open, one little hand flies to her chest, gills flare out, and she leans back theatrically. Then she narrows her eyes into a cheeky, sassy smirk and settles back to her starting pose.
```

### 5. celebrate

```
Action: She bursts with excitement: throws both little arms up, wiggles her whole body side to side, gills bouncing, a huge open-mouthed grin and sparkling eyes. Then she settles back to her starting pose, still beaming.
```

### 6. laugh

```
Action: She laughs hard: eyes squeeze shut, head tips back, shoulders shake, mouth wide open, one little hand half covering her mouth. Then she settles back to a big grin.
```

### 7. sympathy_talk

```
Action: She talks softly and slowly to the camera with a gentle, caring expression: small calm mouth movements, soft eyes, a slow nod partway through. Quiet and warm, like comforting a friend.
```

### 8. wave_hello

```
Action: She notices the viewer, her face lights up, and she gives an enthusiastic little wave with one hand and a big friendly smile, then lowers her hand back to its starting place.
```

### 9. talk_2

```
Action: She chats animatedly to the camera, mouth moving naturally, expressive brows, small hand gestures, a cheeky grin mid-sentence, like she's telling a funny story.
```

### 10. sleepy

```
Action: She gives a big slow yawn, her eyes droop, and she rests her chin on her little hands. Then she blinks herself awake and looks back at the camera with a sheepish smile.
```

## Bin a clip if

- The camera moves, zooms or cuts to another angle (your first clip did all three: a lunge into her face, and the "CAM 2" shot).
- Her look changes: a collar appears, the gills or eyes change, or there are extra fingers or limbs.
- The couch, cushions or window change.

A clip that ends in a different pose from its start is still fine. I can cut out the good stretch and bounce it, like the first five.
