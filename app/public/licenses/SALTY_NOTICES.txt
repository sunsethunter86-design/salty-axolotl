SALTY: CREDITS, LICENCES AND WHAT SHE IS
========================================

Built with Llama.

Salty is an AI. She is a fine-tuned copy of Meta's Llama 3.2 3B Instruct,
named Llama-3.2-3B-Salty-v7a, and she runs entirely on this phone (its
Hexagon NPU). Nothing you say to her leaves the phone.


KNOWN LIMITS (please read)
--------------------------

- She is an AI and can be wrong. She can misremember what you told her, or
  say something with confidence that isn't true.
- She is not a doctor, lawyer, financial adviser or counsellor. Don't rely on
  her for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions.
- She swears and jokes. She is not meant for children.
- If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact emergency
  services (000 in Australia) or Lifeline on 13 11 14.
